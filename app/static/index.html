  1. Log into ums.lpu.in and open your Announcement/Notice Board page.
  2. Press F12 (or right click -> Inspect) and open the Console tab.
  3. Paste the snippet below and press Enter.
  4. Return to SmartNotice -> "Paste Notices" tab, press Ctrl + V, and click Ingest!
